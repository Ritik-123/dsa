Dijkstra's Algorithm:

    . It works when we have positive weight on edges.

    . Dijkstra's Algorithm with negative cycle:

        . Dijkstra Algorithm cannot find a solution with negative cycle.

        . A path is called negative cycle if:

            . There is a cycle (a cycle is a path of edges or vertices where a vertex is reachable from itself)
                Total weight of a cycle is negative number.
