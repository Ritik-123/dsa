Floyd Warshall Algorithm


if D[u][v] > D[u][viaX] + D[viaX] [v]:
    D[u][v] = D[u][viaX] + D[viaX] [v]


1. The vertex is not reachable.

2. Two vertices are directly connected.
    . This is the best solution.
    . It can be imporoved via other vertex.

3. Two vertices are connected via other vertex.

Floyd Warshall with Negative cycle:

    . To go through cycle we need to go negative cycle participating vertex at least twice.

    . Floyd warshall algorithm never runs loop twice via a vertex.

    . Hence, FW can never detect a negative cycle.

Time Complexity: O(V^3)
Space Complexity: O(V^2)
